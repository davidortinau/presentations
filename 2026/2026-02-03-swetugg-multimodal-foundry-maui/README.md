**Swetugg Stockholm 2026** · Lustikulla Konferens & Event, Stockholm, Sweden · Tuesday 3 February 2026, 13:55 · [Session page](https://swetugg-v2-web-app-ava3f0dgcnana0dj.swedencentral-01.azurewebsites.net/Stockholm/2026/sessions/multimodal-apps-with-ai-foundry-and-net-maui)

[![First slide of Multimodal Apps with AI Foundry and .NET MAUI](thumbnail.jpg)](slides.pdf)

## Abstract

> It's easier than ever to develop intelligent apps for mobile and desktop using Azure AI Foundry and .NET. In this session I'll show you how to add support for voice, vision, and more with the power of the latest LLMs and on device models everywhere your app runs across web, mobile, and desktop.
