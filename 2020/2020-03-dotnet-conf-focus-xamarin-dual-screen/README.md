# Developing Dual-screen Experiences with Xamarin

**.NET Conf: Focus on Xamarin** · Online · March 2020 · [Session page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/shows/dotnetconf-focus-on-xamarin/developing-dual-screen-experiences-with-xamarin)

With Guy Merin.

## Abstract

> Join Guy & David as they show off how to get started with Xamarin and building dual-screen experiences for Windows 10x dual-screen devices and Surface Duo with Xamrain.Forms.
>
> They will talk through new controls and APIs just released to help you build beautiful experiences for these devices.
>
> For more information on Xamarin head over to https://aka.ms/XamarinFocus
