# Shaping the Future of .NET MAUI

**MAUI Day Cologne 2024** · Microsoft Office in Cologne, Cologne, Germany · Friday 11 October 2024 · 45 minutes · [Session page](https://mauiday.net/archive/cologne-2024)

## Abstract

> What can you expect to see from .NET MAUI now that we have shipped 4 major releases since Xamarin and .NET Framework? As we have seen so many companies journey through these releases and achieve great production success, we are optimistically setting our sights on what we can do next to help you improve your apps and daily productivity. Join me for a look into potential futures for building client applications with .NET MAUI, and lend your voice to the conversation.
