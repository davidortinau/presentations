# Building Mobile & Desktop Apps with VS and .NET MAUI (T16)

**VS Live! at Microsoft HQ 2025** · Redmond, United States · Tuesday 5 August 2025, 15:00 · 75 minutes · [Session page](https://vslive.com/events/microsofthq-2025/sessions/tuesday/t16-vs-and-net-maui.aspx)

## Abstract

> Visual Studio is the most productive place to build mobile and desktop applications with .NET MAUI. In this session we'll show you how to quickly create and modify your UI at design time, at run time, and optimizing for production to find your ideal blend of speed and app size.
>
> You will learn:
> What Live tools can do for you at design time and run time
> How to structure your solutions and projects to optimize for targeting multiple platforms
> What is new in .NET MAUI for .NET 9 and coming next in .NET 10
